`ServerConfig.worker_startup_failure` lets frameworks opt into fail-loud `pounce.worker.startup` hooks. The default `"ignore"` preserves generic-ASGI compatibility — a hook exception or timeout is logged and serving continues. Setting it to `"shutdown"` makes a hook failure fatal: the worker refuses to accept connections and signals the supervisor (or single-worker server) to shut down instead of serving with uninitialised worker state.
