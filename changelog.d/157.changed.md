`ServerConfig` now documents stability tiers: `worker_mode="subinterpreter"` and the rate-limit, request-queue, introspection, HTTP/3, and observability (`otel_*`/`sentry_*`/`metrics_*`) knobs are marked **beta**, while host/port/workers/timeouts/limits/log_* stay **stable**. `pounce config schema` surfaces the beta tier as an `x-stability` annotation per field (and an `x-stability-values` note on the `worker_mode` subinterpreter value); field names and the field set are unchanged. The `subinterpreter-workers` and `core-contract` design docs were reconciled to the beta wording.
