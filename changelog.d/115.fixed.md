Brought WebSocket-over-HTTP/2 (RFC 8441 Extended CONNECT) to parity with the HTTP/1.1 WebSocket path. The H2 WS handler now enforces `websocket_max_message_size` (a WebSocket `1009` close plus an `RST_STREAM` on an oversize inbound message), guards `websocket.send` against send-before-`accept` / send-after-`close`, negotiates permessage-deflate from the CONNECT headers, and implements the `websocket.http.response.start` / `.body` reject path. The `200` acceptance headers are now deferred until the app accepts so the negotiated `Sec-WebSocket-Extensions` and subprotocol can be echoed.
